# Understand Body Text Tools

Body text is the main descriptive or rules wording on a card. Its field may be called **Card text**, **Rules text**, or **Back text**, depending on the template.

As you type, the card preview updates to show the result. The tools beside the field can help you add symbols, format the wording, choose a text colour, and make longer text fit.

## What tools can I use?

Most body-text fields provide:

- **Insert emoji** to choose and insert a symbol.
- **Insert inline dice** to build a die symbol without typing its formatting code.
- **Formatting help** for emphasis, colour, size, headings, alignment, leader lines, and dice examples.
- A text-colour control when that template supports body-text colour changes.
- **Scale to fit** when the template has a fixed-size text area.

The chosen item is inserted at the cursor. If some text is selected, inserting an emoji or die replaces that selection.

For the complete formatting reference, see [Format Card Text](./format-card-text.md). For picker steps, see [Insert Emoji and Dice](./insert-emoji-and-dice.md).

## Why do the available tools vary?

Each template has its own layout. **Small Artwork**, **Large Artwork**, **Rules**, **Hero Back**, and **Labelled Back** have fixed-size body-text areas, so they can offer **Scale to fit** and can show a **Text clipped** warning.

The **Hero Card** and **Monster Card** use a flexible text area that adjusts as the content changes. They do not offer the same body-text fitting switch or clipping warning. **Logo Back** has no body-text field.

On **Labelled Back**, its body-text tools are available only when back text is enabled.

## Which fitting control should I use?

There are two separate kinds of text fitting:

- **Scale to fit** beside the body-text field applies to that card's fixed body-text area and is saved with the card.
- **Text fitting** in Settings or the preview toolbar controls app-wide minimum sizes and ellipsis preferences for titles and stat headings.

Use [Fit Body Text on a Card](./fit-body-text-on-a-card.md) when the card's descriptive or rules wording is crowded. Use the app-wide setting when titles or stat headings are the problem.

## How much text can I enter?

A body-text field accepts up to 2,000 characters. If the text is longer, shorten it before saving. The character limit and whether the wording visually fits are separate: text below the limit can still be too large for a fixed area.
