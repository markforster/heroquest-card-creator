# General Troubleshooting Questions

These concise answers use the same wording captured during product exploration. Follow the linked guide when you need fuller context or step-by-step instructions.

## What should I try if the downloaded app will not open?

Extract the ZIP, try the operating-system launcher, use macOS Open when required, or fall back to `index.html` and the included README.

[Read the full guidance](../troubleshooting/fix-navigation-download-and-screen-problems.md)

## Why is an expected card control missing?

First check the template: a missing field normally means that layout does not support it. Start another card with the needed template rather than expecting a conversion.

[Read the full guidance](../making-cards/understand-template-specific-card-controls.md)

## How do I recover from an unwanted card-colour change?

Use Revert during the current edit, Discard all unsaved changes when safe, or choose the required colour again after an unwanted value has been saved.

[Read the full guidance](../making-cards/customize-titles-colours-and-copyright.md)

## Why is Scale to fit missing or unavailable?

Scale to fit is absent on flexible Hero and Monster text, Logo Back has no body text, and Labelled Back requires back text to be enabled.

[Read the full guidance](../making-cards/fit-body-text-on-a-card.md)

## What does the Text clipped warning mean?

Text clipped means wording extends beyond a fixed body-text area and is not visible on the rendered card.

[Read the full guidance](../making-cards/fit-body-text-on-a-card.md)

## Why does Text clipped remain after I enable fitting?

If the warning remains after fitting, the content still exceeds the area at the minimum permitted text size.

[Read the full guidance](../making-cards/fit-body-text-on-a-card.md)

## How do I recover when body text does not fit?

Enable fitting, remove unnecessary spacing, shorten the copy, reduce large formatting, or choose a more suitable template.

[Read the full guidance](../troubleshooting/fix-clipped-or-overflowing-card-text.md)

## Can formatting make body text overflow?

Large scale tags, headings, leader layouts, long wording, and extra spacing can all increase the room body text needs.

[Read the full guidance](../making-cards/fit-body-text-on-a-card.md)

## How do I close a stranded Upload Progress window?

Leave and reopen Assets or reload the app, then search for files retained from the cancelled batch.

[Read the full guidance](../troubleshooting/fix-asset-upload-problems.md)

## What is the safest way to retry a partial asset upload?

Find retained files first, then upload only missing files and isolate a repeatedly failing image in its own batch.

[Read the full guidance](../troubleshooting/fix-asset-upload-problems.md)

## In what order should I clear Cards search and filters?

Choose All cards, clear the search, return the face or template filter to All cards, then turn off Not paired.

[Read the full guidance](../troubleshooting/fix-cards-workspace-problems.md)

## Why is Restore unavailable?

Open Recently deleted and select at least one card; Restore is unavailable with no deleted-card selection.

[Read the full guidance](../troubleshooting/fix-cards-workspace-problems.md)

## How can I tell whether a card was moved to Recently deleted or deleted permanently?

**Move to recently deleted** leaves a restore path; **Delete permanently** does not. The normal **Delete** confirmation offers both choices.

[Read the full guidance](../troubleshooting/fix-cards-workspace-problems.md)

## How do I get keyboard shortcuts working again?

Leave the editable field, close the foreground window, return to the owning screen, check the action is available, and press the bare key once.

[Read the full guidance](../reference/keyboard-shortcuts.md)

## What should I do if my browser or operating system uses the same shortcut?

Use the equivalent visible app control; for the template picker, bare N or New avoids a system-level modifier conflict.

[Read the full guidance](../reference/keyboard-shortcuts.md)
